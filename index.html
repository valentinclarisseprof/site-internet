About

I am a mathematics and physics student interested in Lie theory, representation theory, quantum field theory, differential geometry, and mathematical physics.

My work focuses on rigorous mathematical structures behind modern theoretical physics, including quantum symmetries, operator algebras, statistical physics, and geometric methods.
