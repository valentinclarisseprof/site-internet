À propos

I am a mathematics and physics student interested in Lie theory, representation theory, quantum field theory, differential geometry, and mathematical physics.

My work focuses on rigorous mathematical structures behind modern theoretical physics, including quantum symmetries, operator algebras, statistical physics, and geometric methods.

Research Interests

Representation Theory

Lie groups, Lie algebras, unitary representations, Harish-Chandra theory, Poincare group.

Quantum Field Theory

Canonical quantization, renormalization group, gauge theories, spontaneous symmetry breaking.

Mathematical Physics

Differential geometry, operator algebras, thermodynamic geometry, statistical mechanics.
